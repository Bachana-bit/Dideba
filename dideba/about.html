An educational project on the history of Georgia, the country in the Caucasus.

Mission

DIDEBA tells the history of Georgia from ancient Colchis and Iberia to the present day, in English and Georgian where practical.

Editorial principles

  • Established facts, traditional accounts and later interpretations are labelled as such.
  • Dates marked “c.” or “traditional” are approximate or debated.
  • No quotations, documents, troop counts or casualty figures are invented.
  • Where a portrait or photograph is unavailable, a designed placeholder is shown and described as one.
