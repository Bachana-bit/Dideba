Rulers from the Kingdom of Iberia to the Kingdom of Kartli–Kakheti.

This list spans different realms and political structures: the Kingdom of Iberia (Kartli), the medieval Kingdom of Georgia and the later Kingdom of Kartli–Kakheti. It does not imply one continuous unified state. Early reigns rest on medieval chronicles, so their dates are traditional or approximate.
